§ Features

A small, sharp tool — not another platform.

on-device speech

Parakeet v3
runs on your Neural Engine.

Apple Silicon does the heavy lifting. Live transcripts in real time, speaker labels via Sortformer, language detection, all without an API key in sight.

  • Parakeet v3 (INT8)
  • Silero VAD
  • Sortformer diarization
efficient

The mic is always on.
The engine isn't.

A voice activity detector gates the entire pipeline. Parakeet only spins up when someone actually speaks — so there's no constant transcription, no fan noise, no battery drain. Your Mac barely notices NoteTaker is there.

whitelist

System audio only when you say so.

Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack — captured only from apps and tabs you've approved. Browser URL detection, never page content.

storage

Encrypted by default.

Local SQLite + SQLCipher. The key lives in your macOS Keychain and nowhere else.

summaries

Your model, your call.

Claude, GPT, OpenRouter, or run Llama 3.2 3B locally via MLX. Transcripts only travel if you tell them to.

workflow

Session-based review.

Full-text search across every meeting. Rename speakers once and have it stick. Export to Markdown, JSON, or SRT.

open source

Apache 2.0, all the way down.

Read every line. Audit the data flow. Fork it. It's on GitHub.

§ Mechanism

How it works.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Microphone stays open after onboarding. System-audio process tap activates when a whitelisted app or tab is in focus.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Silero VAD chunks the stream. Parakeet runs each utterance on the Neural Engine. Diarization assigns speakers.

  3. 03

    Summarize

    When a session ends, your chosen LLM produces a structured note: key points, decisions, action items, follow-up email.

  4. 04

    Recall

    Everything is encrypted and indexed. Find anything from any meeting in milliseconds.

§ Privacy

We can't see your meetings.
Not the audio, not the transcripts,
not the summaries.

There is no NoteTaker server. There is no NoteTaker account. There's just an app, your Mac, and a Keychain item only your user account can decrypt.

stays on your Mac

  • All audio captured by the mic and system tap
  • Every transcript and speaker label
  • The encryption key in macOS Keychain
  • Crash logs (never uploaded)

leaves only if you ask

  • Transcript text sent to your chosen cloud LLM
  • Nothing else. No telemetry. No analytics.
