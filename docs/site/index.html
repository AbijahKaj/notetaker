How it works

  1. 01

    Capture

    NoteTaker keeps the mic on after setup. When listening is enabled, it also captures audio from whitelisted meeting apps and browser tabs.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Silero VAD chunks the audio into utterances. Parakeet v3 runs them locally on the Neural Engine and writes timestamped text.

  3. 03

    Summarize

    When a session ends, your chosen LLM produces a structured summary with key points, decisions, action items, and a follow-up email draft.

  4. 04

    Search later

    Everything is stored in an encrypted local database with full-text search. Find anything from any meeting in milliseconds.

Privacy you can actually verify.

We can't see your meetings. Not the audio, not the transcripts, not the summaries. There is no NoteTaker server.

What stays on your Mac

  • All audio captured by the mic and system tap
  • Every transcript and speaker label
  • The encryption key (in macOS Keychain)
  • Crash logs (never uploaded)

What leaves only if you ask

  • Transcript text sent to your chosen cloud LLM (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter)
  • Nothing else. No telemetry. No analytics.
