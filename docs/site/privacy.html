Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

NoteTaker is a desktop application that runs entirely on your Mac. There is no NoteTaker backend. There is no NoteTaker account. We — the maintainers — cannot see your meetings, your transcripts, your notes, or your usage of the app, because none of it is ever sent to us.

What NoteTaker captures

  • Microphone audio — Once you grant permission during onboarding, the microphone stays active so that transcription can start instantly when you turn listening on. The audio is fed directly into the on-device speech recognizer.
  • System audio — When listening is on AND a whitelisted meeting app (Zoom, Teams, Slack, etc.) is running, or a whitelisted site (meet.google.com, etc.) is the active browser tab, NoteTaker captures that app's audio via the macOS CoreAudio process tap. System audio is never captured at other times.
  • Browser tab URLs — Only the URL of the active tab is read, and only to detect whether a whitelisted meeting site is open. Page contents are never accessed.

What stays on your Mac

  • All audio captured by NoteTaker
  • All transcripts, speaker labels, and notes
  • The encryption key for your local database (stored in the macOS Keychain)
  • API keys you've added (stored in the macOS Keychain)
  • Crash logs (written locally; never uploaded)
  • Application preferences and meeting/site whitelists

What leaves your Mac (only if you opt in)

  • Transcript text sent to the cloud LLM provider you select (Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter) to generate meeting summaries. Your API key authenticates these requests directly with that provider; NoteTaker is not in the middle.
  • Nothing else. Choose the local MLX provider if you don't want anything to leave your Mac.

Telemetry and analytics

NoteTaker does not collect telemetry, analytics, error reports, usage data, or anonymous identifiers. There is no analytics SDK, no error-reporting service, and no remote logging.

Updates

NoteTaker checks GitHub Releases for new versions on startup and every six hours. These requests go to api.github.com and only share what any HTTP request normally does (your IP, user agent, the repo being queried). GitHub's privacy policy applies to those requests.

Third-party services you may choose to use

If you configure a cloud LLM, your transcript text is sent to that provider's API per their privacy policy:

  • Anthropic
  • OpenAI
  • OpenRouter

Your control

  • Disable listening at any time from the menu bar or hotkey.
  • Remove apps and sites from the whitelists in Settings.
  • Delete sessions individually or wipe the local database from your file system (~/Library/Application Support/NoteTaker).
  • Revoke microphone or system-audio permission in macOS System Settings.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub, or report a security issue through GitHub's private advisories.
